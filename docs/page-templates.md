# 🏙️ TEMPLATE 1: Service × Location (City Pages)

**Used for:** Burlington, Oakville, Milton, Brampton, Mississauga, Halton Hills, Georgetown

## URL Structure
`/weed-delivery-locations/weed-delivery-[city]/`

## Required Sections (in order)

### 1. H1 + Introduction (200-300 words)
- Service area confirmation
- Delivery time promise (60-120 minutes)
- Legal compliance statement (AGCO licensed)
- Local relevance (mention 1-2 specific neighborhoods)

### 2. Why [City] Customers Choose Weedistillery (300-400 words)
- 3 main reasons (speed, discretion, quality)
- Brief explanation of each

### 3. Cannabis Delivery Coverage in [City] (300-400 words)
- Postal code zones (table format)
- Delivery times per zone
- Free delivery threshold

### 4. Popular Products Delivered in [City] (400-500 words)
- Top 5 products (use real products from your menu)
- Quality tier breakdown
- Brief descriptions + price ranges

### 5. Pricing & Current [City] Deals (200-300 words)
- Local promotions
- First-time customer offers
- Bulk pricing tiers

### 6. How to Order Cannabis Delivery in [City] (200-300 words)
- 4-step process (ordered list)
- Payment methods
- Discreet packaging info

### 7. Legal Cannabis Delivery in [City] (300-400 words)
- Age requirement (19+)
- ID verification
- Delivery hours
- Possession limits
- AGCO licensing

### 8. [City] Neighborhoods We Serve (400-500 words)
- 3-5 neighborhoods with unique 1-2 sentence descriptions each
- Each neighborhood: real postal code, local landmarks, typical orders

### 9. Why Choose Cannabis Delivery Over a Dispensary (200-300 words)
- Convenience benefits
- Selection benefits
- Privacy benefits

### 10. Cities Near [City] We Also Deliver To (150-200 words)
- 4-5 cross-links to neighboring city pages

### 11. Frequently Asked Questions (400-500 words)
- 4-6 questions specific to this city
- FAQ schema markup

### 12. About Weedistillery (100-200 words)
- Company overview
- CTA to browse menu

## Word Count Target: 2,000-2,500 words

## Required Schema Markup
- LocalBusiness
- FAQPage
- BreadcrumbList

## Internal Links (5-8 per page)
- Homepage
- Shop
- Contact
- 3-5 related city/neighborhood pages

## Images (5 per page)
1. Hero image (city/regional photo)
2. Coverage map
3. Product flat-lay
4. Discreet packaging
5. Service area map

# 📚 TEMPLATE 3: Use Case / Authority Page

**Used for:** Best Indica for Sleep, Cannabis for Anxiety, Cannabis for Pain Relief

## URL Structure
`/guides/[topic]/`

## ⚠️ CRITICAL COMPLIANCE
- This is EDUCATIONAL content, not medical advice
- No medical claims ("cannabis cures X" = ❌)
- Use language like "Many consumers report..." or "Some research suggests..."
- Include prominent disclaimer at top and bottom

## Required Sections (in order)

### 1. H1 + Disclaimer (300-400 words)
- Topic introduction
- Prominent disclaimer: "This is informational content, not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional."

### 2. Understanding [Topic] (400-500 words)
- Background information
- Why people seek this
- General context

### 3. How Cannabis Interacts with [Topic] (400-500 words)
- Educational explanation
- Mechanism of action (THC, CBD, terpenes)
- Research summary (if available)

### 4. Best Cannabis Products for [Topic] (500-600 words)
- 5-8 products organized by category
- Strain types (indica vs sativa)
- Product formats (flower, edibles, vapes)
- Why each might be relevant

### 5. How to Choose the Right Product (300-400 words)
- Factors to consider
- Dosing guidance
- Format selection

### 6. Safety and Considerations (300-400 words)
- Side effects
- Interactions
- When to avoid

### 7. Frequently Asked Questions (400-500 words)
- 5-7 questions
- Educational tone
- FAQ schema

### 8. Disclaimer + CTA (100-200 words)
- Repeat disclaimer
- Link to relevant products

## Word Count Target: 2,500-3,000 words

## Required Schema Markup
- Article
- FAQPage
- BreadcrumbList

## Internal Links (8-10 per page)
- 5-8 relevant products
- Related use case pages
- Shop, Guides hub

## Images (4-5 per page)
1. Header image (relevant to topic)
2. Product examples
3. Educational infographic (if possible)
4. Comparison chart
5. CTA banner

# 🎨 UNIVERSAL REQUIREMENTS (All Pages)

## Must Have
- [ ] H1 with primary keyword
- [ ] Meta title (under 60 chars)
- [ ] Meta description (under 160 chars)
- [ ] Canonical URL
- [ ] At least 1 image per page with alt text
- [ ] Schema markup appropriate to page type
- [ ] Internal links (3-10 depending on page type)
- [ ] Cannabis compliance: 19+ mention + AGCO reference
- [ ] QA score ≥85/100 before publishing

## Image Guidelines
- WebP format preferred
- Under 100KB per image
- Alt text includes city/topic keyword
- Lazy loading enabled

## Compliance Requirements
- 19+ age requirement mentioned where relevant
- AGCO license reference
- No medical claims
- No marketing to minors
- Discreet packaging mentioned as benefit
