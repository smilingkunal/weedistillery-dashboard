# 🛡️ QUALITY GUIDELINES

## ✅ Good Backlinks
- Cannabis industry sites
- Local news/business sites
- Cannabis directories (Leafly, Weedmaps)
- Cannabis blogs/reviews
- Local community sites
- Educational sites (for guide content)

## ❌ Bad Backlinks (Avoid)
- PBN (Private Blog Network) links
- Link farms
- Spammy directories
- Adult content sites
- Unrelated foreign sites
- Paid links without nofollow

## ⚠️ Monitor for Toxicity
- Sudden spike in low-quality links
- Links from porn/gambling sites
- Links from hacked sites
- Over-optimized anchor text
- Links from countries you don't serve
